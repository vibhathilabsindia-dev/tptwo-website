About RSP

Road Safety Education, Starting in the Classroom.

The Road Safety Patrol was inaugurated in schools in 1981, recognising that road safety habits are best built early. Students in the programme receive practical training in traffic rules, safe road crossing, and responsible behaviour — and take part in supervised road-duty activities alongside their studies, under the motto "Catch Them Young."

Curriculum Focus Areas

Traffic Rules

Safe Crossing

Helmet & Gear

Responsible riding and protective gear habits.

Patrol Duty

Supervised, practical on-ground patrol experience.
