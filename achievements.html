A Legacy of Service

Nearly Five Decades, One Mission.

TPTWO's greatest achievement is its continuity — nearly five decades of uninterrupted volunteer service to Greater Chennai, built by professionals from all walks of life who chose to give their time to public safety. The milestones below trace that journey.

Key Milestones

Our Journey Since 1977

1977

Organisation Founded

Inaugurated under G.O. Ms. No. 2268/Home Department, dated 10 August 1977, as a voluntary body assisting Chennai City Police.

1978

Formal Inauguration

Formally inaugurated at Rajaji Hall, Chennai, by the Governor of Tamil Nadu, in the presence of the Chief Minister.

1981

Road Safety Patrol Launched

RSP inaugurated in schools as a dedicated wing, bringing road safety education to the next generation.

1995

Renamed & Expanded

Renamed under G.O. M.S. No. 1068 and expanded to Coimbatore, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli, Salem, North Arcot, and the Nilgiris.
