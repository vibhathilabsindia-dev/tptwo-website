Why It Matters

Together, We Can Build Safer Roads for Chennai.

The Tamil Nadu Police Traffic Warden Organisation has served Greater Chennai since 1977 — a voluntary force of professionals from all walks of life, trained to assist Greater Chennai Police in traffic regulation, road safety education, and community outreach.

As Chennai continues to grow, so does the need for effective road safety infrastructure and awareness. Corporate partnership can help TPTWO expand the reach and impact of its volunteer-driven mission.

How You Can Help

Partner With Us for a Safer Chennai

We invite businesses and organisations to support TPTWO's road safety mission through Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) partnerships. Your contribution can help expand our reach and strengthen our impact across Greater Chennai. Your support can help us:

  • Provide safety gear and equipment for Traffic Wardens
  • Support Road Safety Patrol training and resources in schools
  • Sponsor public awareness campaigns and events
  • Fund community outreach and junction safety initiatives
